The fixed rules every forecast follows

  1. Freeze the evidence. Everything is fetched into one fingerprinted snapshot. Nothing outside it may be read: no internet, no memory.
  2. Compute in code. Signals and models are plain code; each one lists the snapshot items it used.
  3. Pool with fixed weights. No language model writes the final number; at most it proposes a bounded, cited nudge.
  4. Apply typed human input. Views tilt the number by a capped amount. Documents and watch-outs are evidence for the analysts, never a direct nudge.
  5. Record and verify. Every forecast goes into an append-only, hash-chained ledger and must recompute exactly from its snapshot.
